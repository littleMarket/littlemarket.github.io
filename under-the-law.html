Under the Law

A live look at every law you're standing under, right now.

What it tracks

Under the Law is about layers: federal, state, county, city, and smaller local rules stacked on top of one another, changing the rules of a place one block at a time.

Why it matters

Most rules feel invisible until you cross a boundary. The project makes those boundaries legible without turning the map into legal jargon.
