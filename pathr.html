Pathr

Like Strava for driving. Pathr records your trips, surfaces scenic routes, and builds a personal map of everywhere you've been.

What it does

Pathr records trips in the background, keeps battery use in mind, and turns each drive into a live route you can revisit later.

MVP focus

  • Start, stop, pause, and resume trip recording.
  • See a live polyline as you drive.
  • Browse trip details, distance, duration, and speed.

Design direction

The UI leans into liquid glass: frosted surfaces, soft gradients, and a map-first layout where the route stays the focus.

Privacy note

Trips stay private by default, with share links only for completed drives and controls for hiding start and end locations.

Still in preview, but the goal is simple: make every drive feel worth keeping.
